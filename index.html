WHY THIS MATTERS

The official minimum isn't the same as a comfortable budget.

Canada's IEC program requires eligible participants to demonstrate at least C$2,500 in funds, plus enough money for a return ticket. Your real budget depends on rent, work, food, transport and how quickly you find a job.

This calculator separates one-off arrival costs from normal monthly spending so you can see the difference between surviving and having enough breathing room to enjoy your trip.

Data notes

  • Rent figures are planning estimates anchored to current 2026 asking-rent data and working-holiday accommodation evidence.
  • Staff accommodation varies substantially by employer and availability.
  • Wage estimates are illustrative; actual take-home pay depends on province, tax credits and circumstances.
  • Update assumptions before making major financial or travel decisions.
