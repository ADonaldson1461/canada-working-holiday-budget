STEP 2

Understand the number

The calculator separates startup costs from your monthly burn. That's important: a $20,000 bank balance can look huge until flights, insurance, deposits, gear and your first few weeks without income start eating into it.

Your biggest lever is usually accommodation. If you can secure staff housing, a room share or employer-provided accommodation, your runway can change dramatically.

Planning rule

Don't treat Canada's official CAD $2,500 proof-of-funds requirement as your target. It is a government minimum for arrival, not a recommendation for a comfortable move.
