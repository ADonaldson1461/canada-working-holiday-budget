SAFETY

Don't get caught by a fake rental.

  • Never send a deposit simply because a listing looks cheap.
  • Verify the property, landlord and tenancy terms.
  • Be cautious of pressure to pay by unusual methods.
  • Prefer viewing in person or using a reputable platform where possible.
  • Ask your employer whether staff accommodation exists before signing a private lease.
