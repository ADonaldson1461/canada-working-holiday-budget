Monthly fare capping Monthly fare capping lets you pay as you go and automatically stop paying TTC fares once you reach the monthly cap. Tap the same payment method every time. Once you reach 47 eligible TTC fares in a calendar month, the rest of your TTC trips that month are free. Monthly fare capping resets on the first day of each calendar month. Monthly fare capping applies to eligible fares paid with: PRESTO card, including a physical card or PRESTO in mobile wallet Interac&reg; Debit card Credit card Cards in a mobile wallet Monthly fare capping does not apply to: Cash fares PRESTO Tickets Paper transfers Using different cards, devices or mobile wallets may prevent your fares from counting toward the same monthly cap. For example, tapping a physical credit card for one trip and the same card in a mobile wallet for another trip may count as different payment methods. Learn more about monthly fare capping . Pay with cash Cash fare payment is available on buses, streetcars and Line 5 Eglinton and Line 6 Finch West surface station platforms. Exact fare is required and no change is provided. To pay with cash: On buses : deposit exact cash into the fare box and ask the Operator for a paper transfer. On streetcars : deposit exact cash into the Fares and Transfers Machine and take your paper transfer. On Line 5 Eglinton and Line 6 Finch West surface station platforms : deposit cash into a Fares and Transfers Machine and take your paper transfer. Cash fares are for continuous, one‑way trips only and do not include a two‑hour transfer. Cash fares do not count toward monthly fare capping. Always take a paper transfer as Proof‑of‑Payment. You can also use cash at a Fare Vending Machine in stations and on Line 5 Eglinton and Line 6 Finch West surface station platforms to buy a PRESTO ticket or to purchase/load a PRESTO card. Tap the same payment method for every trip. Once you reach 47 eligible TTC fares in a calendar month, the rest of your TTC trips that month are free. Monthly fare capping resets on the first day of every calendar month.&nbsp; Which payment methods count toward monthly fare capping? Monthly fare capping applies to eligible TTC fares paid with: PRESTO card, including a physical card or PRESTO in mobile wallet Interac&reg; Debit card Credit card Cards in a mobile wallet Cash fares and PRESTO Tickets do not count toward monthly fare capping.&nbsp; Do I need to buy a monthly pass to get unlimited rides? No. With monthly fare capping, you do not need to buy most TTC monthly passes upfront. Tap the same payment method every time, and once you reach the 47-fare cap, the rest of your TTC trips in that calendar month are free. Adult 12-Month Passes and Post-Secondary Student Monthly Passes are still available. What happens if I use more than one card or device? You must tap the same card or device every time for your fares to count toward the same monthly cap. A physical card and the same card in a mobile wallet count as different payment methods. If you switch between cards, devices or mobile wallets, your fares will not count toward the same cap. Do two-hour transfers count toward monthly fare capping? Only paid fares count toward monthly fare capping. Trips taken during your two-hour transfer window are not charged another TTC fare and do not count as an additional fare toward the monthly cap. Monthly and 12-Month passes What happened to the TTC Monthly and 12-Month Passes? Most TTC monthly passes were discontinued on August 31, 2026, when monthly fare capping came into effect. Adult 12-Month Passes and Post-Secondary Student Monthly Passes are still available. What happens if my monthly pass doesn&rsquo;t work? Contact PRESTO at 1‑877‑378‑6123. If you don&rsquo;t have funds available on your PRESTO card, load money at a Fare Vending Machine or pay by another method until the pass is available.&nbsp; Can my PRESTO card have both a pass and a cash balance? Yes. You can use the non-pass balance on any of the ten other transit agencies in the GTHA and Ottawa using PRESTO.&nbsp; How does Autorenew work? Autorenew is available for TTC 12-Month passes and allows your pass to be automatically renewed each year. You can set up and manage Autorenew through your My PRESTO Account at prestocard.ca , or by mail using the Autorenew form from the PRESTO Paper Forms page . You only need to sign up once. Autorenew can be set up online using a credit card or Visa Debit. If you would like to set up your Autorenew using your bank account (pre-authorized debit), please use a paper form. Please note: Autorenew is no longer available for discontinued TTC Monthly Passes. Can I load a Monthly Pass when ordering a PRESTO card online? Yes, eligible customers can load a Post-Secondary Student Monthly Pass on their PRESTO card, but you must activate the card after it arrives. Activation requires the 17-digit PRESTO number and PIN.&nbsp; Can I cancel my Monthly or 12-Month pass for a partial refund? No. Monthly passes expire at the end of each month; 12-Month passes expire at the end of each year. Refunds are not provided for mid-month or mid-year cancellation. With monthly fare capping, customers using eligible pay-as-you-go fares only pay up to the monthly cap based on actual usage. There is no upfront monthly pass payment to refund if the cap is not reached. PRESTO cards What if I lose my PRESTO card? If your PRESTO card is registered to a My PRESTO Account, you may be able to cancel it and transfer the balance or pass to a new card. Visit PRESTO's Lost or Damaged Cards page or contact PRESTO at 1-877-378-6123 to learn more.&nbsp; Transfers Do other transit systems accept TTC paper transfers if I paid cash? Most agencies do not accept TTC paper transfers. MiWay does not accept TTC paper transfers except &ldquo;Zone 3&rdquo; punched transfers from TTC Route 52 Lawrence. YRT does not accept TTC paper transfers except &ldquo;Zone 3&rdquo; punched transfers from TTC Routes 17 Birchmount, 68 Warden, 102 Markham Road, 129 McCowan North, and 160 Bathurst North.
